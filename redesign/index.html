About

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at ShareTrip in Dhaka, where I lead the Public API team. We build and maintain the gateways and the business logic behind flights, hotels, the e-commerce shop, mobile recharge and utility bill payments, as well as visa and holidays. That covers the supplier integrations and the pricing behind every booking: markups, discounts, coupons and add-ons, so each customer is charged the right amount.

As team lead, I plan the team's work, review its code and take changes from staging to production. I keep these flows fast and safe: I profile slow queries to remove wasted database calls, and I roll out security patches and runtime upgrades across our services.
